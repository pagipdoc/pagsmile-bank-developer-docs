Choose a managed-account model before you open an account through the Pagsmile Open API. The model determines the parent account and whether you submit separate compliance information.

## Compare models

MA is the master account. MOR, VA and PA are the models you select when you [create a managed account](/api-reference/onboarding/create-managed-account).

| Model | Direct parent | Compliance submission |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MOR, operating account | Owning MA | No separate submission |
| VA, managed account | Owning MA | Account holder's own information |
| PA, payment account | Owning MA or its VA | Account holder's own information |

Select MOR, VA or PA below to read the model-specific rules.

## Account hierarchy

Your USER credential defines the owner scope. Within that scope, the MA is the root account; MOR and VA link directly to it.

A PA has one direct parent: the owning MA or a VA directly under that MA. A MOR or another PA cannot be its parent.

## Before you call

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="ACCOUNT" icon="building-columns">
    <Warning>ACCOUNT credentials cannot create managed accounts. Use the owning USER credential for account opening.</Warning>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="USER" icon="user">
    Use the USER credential that owns the MA. Managed-account functionality must be enabled for that USER.

    The USER must have exactly one active MA. That MA must also have an active bank account.

    Account opening is USER-scoped. Omit `X-ISPB` and `X-Account-Number`; the creation flow resolves the owning MA.
  </Tab>

    <Warning>PLATFORM credentials cannot create managed accounts. Use the owning USER credential instead.</Warning>
  </Tab>

Account creation can fail if the applicable opening fee cannot be charged. Confirm funding before you submit the request.

## Open a MOR

Use MOR for an operating account under the owning MA. You do not submit a separate compliance review for this model.

Send `reference_id` and select MOR in `model`. You can also provide a trade name and notification email.

Do not send compliance inputs, a return URL or `linked_account`. The creation flow selects the owning MA as the direct parent.

See [Create managed account](/api-reference/onboarding/create-managed-account) for the MOR request variant.

</View>

<View title="VA" icon="building">

## Open a VA

Use VA for a managed account with its own account-holder information. The creation flow links it directly to the owning MA.

Send `reference_id`, select VA in `model`, and choose a `compliance_mode`. Provide the account holder's document type, taxpayer ID and legal name.

The selected compliance mode determines the additional review information you submit. Do not send `linked_account` for VA.

See [Create managed account](/api-reference/onboarding/create-managed-account) for the VA request variant and compliance modes.

</View>

<View title="PA" icon="wallet">

## Open a PA

Use PA for a payment account with its own account-holder information. The compliance payload supports an individual CPF or a business CNPJ.

Send `reference_id`, select PA in `model`, and choose a `compliance_mode`. Provide the account holder's document type, taxpayer ID and name.

You must also send `linked_account` with both `ispb` and `account_number`. These identify the PA's direct parent, not the new PA.

The parent must belong to the same USER and be the owning MA or a VA directly under it. Its identity and bank account must be active.

For PA account operations, use USER credentials and target the PA's own bank account with `X-ISPB` and `X-Account-Number`. Include both headers in the [signature](/environments-and-credentials#sign-requests).

See [Create managed account](/api-reference/onboarding/create-managed-account) for the PA request variant.

</View>
